## Hey, I'm Rocky Mishra

**Software Engineer | Backend Developer | MS in AI Student**

I'm a backend engineer with 6+ years of experience building scalable systems with C#, .NET, SQL, and Azure. My work spans fintech, microfinance, and identity management — integrating payment gateways like UnionPay, Xendit, and Inpay, driving microservice migrations, and modernizing legacy .NET applications to .NET 6+.

Currently pursuing a **Master's in Artificial Intelligence** at BTU Cottbus, Germany, while working on backend infrastructure at **SPHERE**, a US-based identity hygiene management company.

Previously built backend systems at **InfoDevelopers** and **Inficare** in Nepal.
